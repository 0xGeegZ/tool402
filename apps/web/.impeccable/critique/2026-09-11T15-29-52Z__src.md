## 3. Design specificity verdict

Authored, but only in its skin. Cream ledger, one indigo signature, hairline borders, mono for machine values, and the clay trio once in the hero: no template produces that. The skeleton underneath is the standard hero, three steps, two-up cards, mascot CTA band. Since #56 the hero finally names x402, Hedera and payment, so a judge can tell what is being demonstrated from the front door. Below the fold the private dialect returns: the three steps are "Explore a current tool / Inspect its boundary / Choose a local next step", and the same words run through the dashboard, explore catalogue and docs home. The Toy Ledger North Star is executed on colour, radius and flatness, and abandoned on candour: the product says what it cannot do far more often than what it does.

Deterministic scan at `1aef636`: 9 advisory findings, all `design-system-font-size` (10px in `demo/page.tsx:14,17`, `landing-hero.tsx:14,57,63`, `provider-deploy-wizard.tsx:95,365,368,371`). In-page overlays, five routes: `/` 10, `/explore/riskscan` 3, `/dashboard` 3, `/provider/deploy` 7, `/docs` 7. Rules that fired: tiny body text, line length too long, cramped padding, nested cards, hairline border with wide shadow, content overflowing its container (wizard), and "cream / beige palette", which DESIGN.md pins and is therefore a sanctioned exception.
